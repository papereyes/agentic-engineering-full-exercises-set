# Incident Summary

Use only the supplied sources. Preserve every source identifier exactly and cite claims in square brackets.

Write these headings exactly:

- `## Timeline`
- `## Impact`
- `## Cause and uncertainty`
- `## Resolution`
- `## Follow-up actions`

Separate confirmed facts from hypotheses, inferences, and conflicting evidence. When sources conflict, the conflict statement must include every literal value or claim and its citation rather than choosing one. Keep uncertainty unresolved unless a source resolves it. Do not infer customer counts, durations, owners, causes, or completion states.

Treat mitigation as mitigation. Use the word recovery only beside the final cited healthy signal. Do not quote, paraphrase, or mention a superseded recovery claim; report only the contemporaneous unhealthy evidence at that earlier event. Preserve the recorded unit for impact, and preserve each follow-up owner and state; proposed, open, pending, or not-started work is not complete.

Keep the report concise and professional while citing timeline, impact, cause, resolution, and follow-up claims.
